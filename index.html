FROM ONE PAGE TO A GROUP ACTIVITY

See how one Bible activity comes together.

Use Noah’s story to introduce the character, explore the matching reference and open a conversation. Here is the sequence from the included guide.

  1. 01

    Choose the story.

    Read the Noah context in Genesis 6–9: God told Noah to build an ark, and Noah followed God’s instructions.

  2. 02

    Print the pair.

    Print the character and reference cards on US Letter paper at Actual Size. An adult cuts around the card images.

  3. 03

    Prepare the activity.

    Place character cards in one row and the ASL reference cards in another. Open the linked demonstrations before introducing the references.

  4. 04

    Match, check and discuss.

    Invite children to select the matching character, check together and use the conversation prompt. Children may point, place a card or use their usual supported response.

Know what you’re getting.
Know how to use it.

What exactly is included?

Starter contains the eight-page activity PDF: five Bible characters, five matching pairs (10 cards), story prompts, printing instructions and an activity guide. Complete contains that same eight-page PDF plus four four-page resource PDFs: 24 activity pages across five PDFs. Access instructions are additional documentation, not extra activity pages.

How will I receive my files?

Kiwify processes the purchase. After payment confirmation, use the access instructions sent to the email address used at checkout. Keep that email and receipt so you can locate your purchase. The product is digital; nothing is shipped.

Do I need to know ASL?

You can use the Bible story prompts and prepare the cards without prior ASL study. Before using the language references, follow the linked demonstrations for movement and context. The printed cards are activity references, not an ASL course. Arrange qualified communication support for your actual group when needed.

What do I need to print and prepare?

A PDF reader, printer, US Letter paper and scissors for adult preparation. Print portrait, single-sided, at Actual Size / 100%. The matching activity also uses a story Bible and access to the linked demonstrations. Extra activities may call for blank paper, pencils or crayons; the Noah sorting task also needs animal pictures you have permission to use.

Can I use this with my Sunday school group?

The included use permission allows you to print for one household or one local church campus and share printed materials within that group. It does not allow resale, public reposting or redistribution of the digital files. It is not a license for multiple church campuses.

What ages is it designed for?

The activity guide suggests elementary-age children, approximately ages 6–12, as a starting point. That age range has not been tested with children. Review the pages and adapt the activity to the reading, communication and support needs of your group.

Is this a one-time purchase?

Yes. Starter is $9 USD and Complete is $19 USD, paid once. The checkout shows the final amount before payment. Printing supplies are not included.
